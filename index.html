Frequently asked questions

How does the timer work?

Drag the dial to choose how long to focus, then tap Start. When the focus ends you can take a break; after a few sessions you get a longer one. If you keep going past the end, the timer counts the overtime until you move on. You can change the lengths in Menu.

What does Premium include?

SimplePomodoro Premium is a monthly subscription that adds more clock designs, your own sounds, break animations and iCloud sync. The timer, stats, categories and the built-in sounds are free.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Menu and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, open Menu and tap Manage Subscription. You can also go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or App Store › your account › Subscriptions on the Mac. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

How does iCloud sync work?

With Premium, turn on Sync with iCloud in Menu on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on. The running timer, its category, the durations and your stats then stay in step across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Changes usually arrive within a few seconds.

Why do sounds only play on one of my devices?

With sync on, the focus and break sounds, chimes and overtime ticking play on the device you used last, so two devices don't play over each other. The other devices still ring the 1-minute warning bell and the phase-end alerts. Start, stop or adjust the timer on a device to move the sounds there.

I don't get the phase-end alerts.

Check that notifications are allowed for SimplePomodoro: Settings › Notifications on iPhone and iPad, or System Settings › Notifications on the Mac. A Focus mode or silent mode can also hold them back. Muting all sounds in the app silences the alerts too.

Where are the sounds I import?

Sounds you import stay on the device where you imported them. They aren't uploaded anywhere, including iCloud.
