Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

In short

SimplePomodoro doesn't collect any data about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account.

What the app keeps, and where

On your device

Your timer settings, categories, the history of your focus sessions and breaks (for Stats), your sound preferences, and any sounds you import. Imported sounds stay on the device where you imported them.

In your iCloud account, only if you turn on sync

With SimplePomodoro Premium and Sync with iCloud turned on, the running timer, its category and the durations are kept in iCloud key-value storage, and your session history and categories in your private iCloud database (CloudKit). This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to it. You can turn sync off at any time in Menu, and delete the app's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app checks your subscription status on your device and never sees your payment details.

Notifications and Live Activities

Phase-end alerts and Live Activities are created on your device. They aren't sent through any server of ours.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code, analytics, advertising or tracking. It doesn't share or sell any data, because it doesn't collect any.

Children

SimplePomodoro is suitable for everyone and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
